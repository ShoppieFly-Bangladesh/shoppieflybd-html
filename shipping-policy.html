Doorstep Parcel Inspection Guarantee

At ShoppieFlyBD, we believe in 100% transparency. When your order arrives via courier:

  • You have the full right to open the external packaging in front of the delivery rider before handing over payment for Cash on Delivery.
  • Verify that the product model, color, and packaging match your purchase order.
  • If the package is visibly damaged, broken, or contains an incorrect item, you can refuse to accept it on the spot with zero penalties.
  • Please note: Product power-on testing or software setup is not included during rider handover. For defective items discovered after unboxing, our 7-Day Replacement Policy applies.

Multi-Store Orders & Package Splitting

Because ShoppieFlyBD is a multi-vendor ecosystem hosting verified official merchants nationwide:

  • If your shopping cart contains items from multiple different stores (e.g. Ocean Crate and Fashion Hub), the items will be dispatched directly from each seller's respective warehouse for maximum speed.
  • Your items may arrive in separate parcel packages with individual courier tracking codes.
  • You will receive real-time SMS notifications for each package shipment with its consignment code.
